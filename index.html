Hermes integration

A private, non-commercial automation connecting its owner’s Google Workspace account with a locally operated Hermes assistant.

Purpose

This integration lets the account owner request useful actions involving Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts. It is maintained for personal use and is not offered as a public service.

There are no public accounts, subscriptions, advertisements, or data sales. Access is limited to the owner’s Google account and only occurs after the owner grants permission through Google OAuth.

How access is used

Google data is used only to carry out owner-requested tasks, such as reading relevant messages, reviewing a calendar, or working with the owner’s files. The integration does not use Google data for advertising, profiling, or training public machine-learning models.
